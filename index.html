In recent decades, deforestation has become a pressing issue in New Zealand, triggering concerns among environmentalists, communities, and government agencies alike. New Zealand's lush forests, once teeming with diverse flora and fauna, have faced significant threats due to human activities. Understanding the major causes behind this phenomenon is essential for conservation and environmental sustainability.

One of the primary machines utilized in deforestation is the feller buncher, known for its efficiency in cutting down trees. This equipment combines the functions of a tree cutter and a harvester, allowing multiple trees to be cut and gathered quickly.

How Deforestation Has Affected Wildlife

Deforestation removes animal habitats and threatens countless species. Approximately 80% of the world's land-based species live in forests. The loss of trees removes food, shelter, and water sources while fragmenting habitats, making it difficult for animals to survive and reproduce. Species such as koalas, pandas, and tigers have suffered major population declines due to habitat destruction.
